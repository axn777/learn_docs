## OpenMMLab

[OpenMMLab](https://github.com/open-mmlab)  builds the most influential open-source computer vision algorithm system in the deep learning era. It aims to

- provide high-quality libraries to reduce the difficulties in algorithm reimplementation
- create efficient deployment toolchains targeting a variety of backends and devices
- build a solid foundation for computer vision research and development
- bridge the gap between academic research and industrial applications with full-stack toolchains

## SAMURAI 

SAMURAI（SAM-based Unified and Robust zero-shot visual tracker with Motion-Aware Instance-level memory）是华盛顿大学基于 SAM2 改进的专用零样本视觉目标跟踪（VOT）模型，核心差异在于SAM2 面向通用分割，SAMURAI 聚焦跟踪并强化运动感知与记忆选择。
